How the full screen works

  1. 01Open the YouTube app once, then close it, so it is warm in memory.
  2. 02Every channel here points at youtube.com/redirect?q=…, which hands the tap to the YouTube app — and that app draws over the whole screen.
  3. 03YouTube shows a leaving YouTube interstitial. Tap Go to site and the channel loads inside that full-screen view.
  4. 04Video only plays in Park. YouTube is linked directly — it is already the full-screen app.
